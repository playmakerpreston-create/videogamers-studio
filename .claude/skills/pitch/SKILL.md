# /pitch <idea>

1. Spawn `writer` in **Mode A (rework)** with the owner's idea verbatim (`$ARGUMENTS`). It saves `spec.json` + `copy.md` to `output/<YYYY-MM-DD>/<slug>/`.
2. Show the owner the chosen hook, the slide-by-slide copy, the alternate angle and the caption. Keep it tight.
3. Spawn `designer` to render. Show slide 1 and list any placeholder images needed.

If the owner says "go with the alternate", have Writer swap it in and re-render.
