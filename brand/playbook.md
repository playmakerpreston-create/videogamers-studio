# The Playbook: how every videogamers post is built

Learned from 5 top @stonks posts (`brand/examples/stonks-1..5.webp`). **Every agent reads this before working.** Every post must go through the thought process below.

---

## 1. What the 5 examples teach

| # | Post | Likes | The mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | "a perfect night in 2012 / 2026" | **249K** | **Same core, aged accessories.** The night didn't change (BO2, pizza, Doritos, 11:47pm). Only the props got older: Xbox 360 → PS5, Disney XD → ZYN, Turtle Beach → wireless, + Epsom salt and a search for "best settings for adults nba2k". |
| 1 | "2013 / 2026" GTA V → GTA VI | **192K** | **Same want, the obstacle disappears.** 2013: begging mom ("it's a racing game"). 2026: "babe, can you hand me my wallet?" The quote is the punchline. |
| 4 | "my parents in 2008 / my dumb ahh at the book fair" | 83K | **Adult crisis vs kid obliviousness.** The reader recognizes being the kid, now understands the parents. |
| 5 | "the problem was never the price" | 74K | **The flip.** "i have all summer but i don't have $60" → "i have $60 but i don't have 45 minutes." Time and money swap places. |
| 3 | "the american dream 2006 / 2026" | 33.7K | **The weakest one.** It's general social commentary (house, pension) rather than the reader's own childhood. Not specific to *one night* or *one game*. **Lesson: personal nostalgia beats big-picture commentary.** |

**What the winners share:** the reader *was* the kid in the top panel and *is* the adult in the bottom panel. The thing he loves (gaming) never changed. Everything around it did.

## 2. The thought process (do this every time, in order)

1. **Pick the constant.** What never changed? (He still loves GTA. He still plays at 11:47pm. He still wants the boys online.)
2. **Pick the two years.** A childhood year tied to a real game moment (2009 MW2, 2011 MW3/Skyrim, 2012 BO2, 2013 GTA V, 2017–18 Fortnite Ch1) vs **2026**.
3. **Name the variable.** Exactly one thing flips or ages: money ↔ time, permission ↔ energy, mom ↔ girlfriend, Xbox 360 ↔ PS5, Mtn Dew ↔ White Monster, staying up ↔ falling asleep in the lobby.
4. **Load the props.** 6–10 hyper-specific objects per panel. **Each prop is its own micro-joke** (e.g. a phone search bar reading "best settings for adults nba2k", a DraftKings line "won $18.42 last night, all-time -$3,806"). Pair them across the panels where possible (same couch, same Doritos, different everything else).
5. **Write the header.** Short, lowercase, parallel: "a perfect night in 2012:" / "a perfect night in 2026:". The same words top and bottom, only the year changes.
6. **Write the quote** (optional, but it's the strongest move). One line in his voice under each panel, in quotation marks. The bottom quote is the punchline.
7. **Write the caption** (see section 4). It's a second joke, not a summary.
8. **Run the gates** (section 6).

## 3. The visual formula (designer)

- **Layout:** one image, two stacked panels, split by a thin black line. Each panel has a bold black header with the year, then the illustration, then an optional quote. White background.
- **Character:** wojak-style cartoon guy. Simple bold black outlines, flat colors, pale face. The kid wears a flat-brim cap, a bright tee (Nike/KD energy) and sits cross-legged on the floor or a beanbag. The adult has a beard, bags under his eyes, a Patagonia-style vest or hoodie, a gaming chair or couch, and a tired half-smile.
- **Props:** a flat-lay arrangement around the character, clearly separated and readable at phone size. Real era-accurate consoles, cases, snacks and drinks.
- **Same composition both panels** so the eye compares item by item.
- **Text is rendered by us, not by the AI** (headers, quotes and small labels), so spelling is always correct. The AI draws the picture only.
- Instagram labels these "AI content" (stonks has it too). Leave it on.

## 4. The caption formula (writer)

Stonks captions are **long (100–200 words)** and are a second, deeper joke. The structure:

1. **Opening line in persona voice.** Stonks = finance-bro jargon about childhood ("lacked the necessary liquidity to complete the acquisition without securing funding from Mom").
   **videogamers = gamer/patch-notes jargon about adult life:** "nerfed", "stamina bar", "respawn timer", "side quests", "XP", "ping", "patch notes", "main quest", "AFK", "build", "DLC", "server maintenance".
   > "In 2013, the main quest was getting GTA V without a parent reading the back of the case. In 2026, the only side quest standing between me and Leonida is a 9am standup."
2. **Ultra-specific numbers and times.** 11:52, 7:06, $11.40, "three games", "45 minutes". Odd numbers are funnier than round ones.
3. **The escalation:** a run of specific adult-life items (ZYNs, Excel open so everyone assumes you're working, Epsom salt, falling asleep in the lobby).
4. **The emotional turn** (one sincere-ish line): "He didn't outgrow the perfect night. The perfect night simply became more expensive."
5. **Parody disclaimer ending.** Stonks: "Past performance does not guarantee future results." **Ours: ESRB/game-legal parody.**
   - "Rated M for Mature. Online interactions not rated by the ESRB."
   - "Results may vary based on server load and how many meetings you have tomorrow."
   - "Progress will not be saved."
   - "Do not turn off your console while the 25-year-old is saving."
6. **CTA line:** "Follow @videogamers for more ___" (rotate it: "…for more content you'll see at 1am instead of sleeping", "…we're also supposed to be asleep").
7. **Hashtags (4–5):** #videogamers #gamingmemes #nostalgia + 1–2 specific (#gta6 #blackops2 #fortnite #cod).

## 5. How we're different from stonks (don't become a copy)

- Stonks = **money** lens on nostalgia. Videogamers = **gaming** lens. Our props and references go *deeper into the games*: the BO2 lobby screen, Halo Reach, Xbox Live Gold cards, Mtn Dew Game Fuel, Turtle Beach X12, the PS3 fan noise, Clash builders, Fortnite Ch1 map, Season X.
- Stonks already did "perfect night 2012", "GTA V → VI wallet", "$60 vs 45 minutes". **Never repeat their premise.** Reuse the mechanism with a new constant.
- Fresh mechanisms are welcome: ranked/tier lists, countdowns, workday timelines (see `voice.md`). But the then-vs-now split is our anchor format, about 2 of the 3 daily posts.

## 6. Gates (every post must pass all of them)

- [ ] **Group chat test:** he'd send it with no caption.
- [ ] **He was the kid AND is the adult.** If only one panel is relatable, kill it.
- [ ] **One constant, one variable.** Not a list of grievances.
- [ ] **6+ specific, era-accurate props** per panel (verify release years with a search).
- [ ] **Gaming is the subject**; work and money are the props.
- [ ] **Not a stonks premise** (check `brand/examples/` and `brand/formats.md`).
- [ ] **The caption is its own joke**, written in patch-notes voice, ending in a disclaimer and a CTA.

## 7. Carousel extension (we post carousels)

- **Slide 1 = the then-vs-now image.** It must work alone; most likes come from it.
- **Slides 2–4 = bonus beats,** each one pushing the joke further: a "2030 (projected)" panel, a zoom-in on one prop's micro-joke, a "patch notes: 25-year-old build" list, or a different year's version.
- **Last slide = the realest line** (bigtext), or a countdown when it's a GTA 6 post.
