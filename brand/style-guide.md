# videogamers: Style Guide
*For the wojak generator, and for anyone or anything making posts for @videogamers.*

---

## 0. Paste-in version (short, for prompt fields)

```
PAGE: @videogamers, a meme page about gaming as a tired 25-year-old adult. Inspired by @stonks, but the subject is gaming, not money.
VOICE: a 25-year-old guy who grew up on COD (MW2/BO2), Assassin's Creed (Ezio), Fortnite Chapter 1 and Clash of Clans. He hates his job, counts down to logging on, and has bills, but still wants a headset, the boys online, and nowhere to be tomorrow. GTA 6 (Nov 19, 2026) is his most anticipated game ever. Humor = something he'd send the group chat: nostalgic, specific, relatable, a little exhausted. Lowercase, short, never explains the joke.
FORMAT: two stacked panels. Top = childhood year (2009–2014), bottom = 2026. The same bold black lowercase header on both, only the year changes ("a perfect night in 2012:" / "a perfect night in 2026:"). Optional quote under each panel; the bottom quote is the punchline.
ART: wojak-style cartoon, clean bold black outlines, flat colors, pure white background, pale wojak faces. Kid: flat-brim cap, bright tee, cross-legged on carpet, wide happy face. Adult: short beard, dark circles, red-tired eyes, quarter-zip/fleece vest or gray hoodie, tired half-smile. Each panel has 6–10 era-accurate props in a clean flat-lay around him. Same composition in both panels so viewers compare item by item.
RULE: one thing never changes (he loves gaming). One thing flips (money↔time, mom↔girlfriend, Xbox 360↔PS5, staying up↔falling asleep in the lobby). Every prop is a mini joke.
```

## 1. Who the page is

**One sentence:** gaming memes for the guy who grew up in MW2 lobbies and now has a 9am standup.

**The narrator** is 25, born ~2001. He:
- grew up on **COD** (MW2 2009, BO1 2010, MW3 2011, BO2 2012), **Assassin's Creed** (the Ezio trilogy), **Fortnite** (Chapter 1, 2017–2019) and **mobile** (Clash of Clans, Clash Royale, Subway Surfers)
- has a job he hates and spends the workday waiting to get on
- has rent, a car payment and a 401k he doesn't understand
- still wants a headset, the boys online, and nowhere to be tomorrow
- has been waiting for **GTA 6** since he was 12. It's his most anticipated game of all time. **It releases Thursday, Nov 19, 2026.**

**The test for every post:** *Would he send it to the group chat with no caption?*

**Gaming is the subject. Work and money are the setting.** If it's a work joke with a controller in it, it's wrong.

## 2. Voice

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| lowercase, group-chat cadence | Title Case Marketing Voice |
| hyper-specific: "Rust", "11:47pm", "47.3 GB" | general: "old games", "late at night" |
| tired, nostalgic, affectionate | bitter, preachy, "kids these days" |
| let the image carry the joke | explaining it, "lol", "relatable?", "tag a friend" |
| real game names, real years, real maps | made-up or wrong details (this crowd will notice) |
| laugh *with* gamers | punch down at women, casuals, mobile players, kids |
| 0 emojis on the image | emoji-heavy |

**Slang that fits:** "the boys", "get on", "locked in", "cooked", "gg", "one more game", "be on at 9", "bro".
**Banned:** "gamers rise up", "epic", "pwned", "noob" (unironically), "we've all been there", "POV:" more than once a week.

---

## 3. The post formula (how every image is built)

1. **The constant:** the thing that never changed. He still loves gaming, still plays at midnight, still wants the boys on.
2. **The two years:** a childhood year tied to a real game moment, vs **2026**.
3. **The variable:** exactly ONE thing flips or ages.
   - money ↔ time ("i have all summer but no $60" → "i have $60 but no 45 minutes")
   - permission ↔ energy (mom says no → his body says no)
   - mom ↔ girlfriend
   - 12/12 friends online ↔ 1 online (it's him)
   - Xbox 360 ↔ PS5, Mtn Dew ↔ Monster Ultra, carpet ↔ gaming chair with lumbar support
4. **The props:** 6–10 per panel, era-accurate, and **each one is its own mini joke** (e.g. a phone search bar reading "best settings for adults 2k26", a Teams status kept green by a mouse jiggler).
5. **The header:** the same words top and bottom, only the year changes.
6. **The quote:** one line under each panel, in quotation marks, in his voice. The bottom one is the punchline.

**Benchmarks from @stonks:** the best performers (249K, 192K) were about *one specific night* or *one game*. The worst (33K) was general social commentary. **Personal and specific beats big-picture every time.**

## 4. Layout

**Canvas:** 1080 × 1350 px (4:5 portrait, Instagram feed). 1080 × 1080 is acceptable.

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ a perfect night in 2012:    │  ← header, bold black, lowercase, left or centered
│                             │
│   [ illustration: kid +     │
│     6–10 props, flat-lay ]  │
│                             │
│   "mom it's just one more   │  ← optional quote, bold, centered
│    game"                    │
├─────────────────────────────┤  ← 4–6px solid black divider
│ a perfect night in 2026:    │
│                             │
│   [ same composition,       │
│     adult + aged props ]    │
│                             │
│   "i'm going to sleep i     │  ← punchline quote
│    swear"                   │
└─────────────────────────────┘
                 @videogamers  ← small, bottom right, 55% opacity
```

- **Same composition in both panels:** character in the same spot, same angle, props mirrored where possible.
- Leave breathing room. Every prop must read at phone size.
- The handle `@videogamers` goes bottom right, small and gray, never big.

## 5. Typography

| Element | Font | Weight | Size (on 1080 wide) | Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Panel header | Inter / Helvetica Neue / Arial | Black (900) or ExtraBold (800) | 80–100 px | lowercase (years in numerals) |
| Quote | same | Bold (700) | 54–64 px | lowercase, in curly quotes " " |
| Prop labels / mini text | same | SemiBold (600) | 22–32 px | as it'd appear in real life |
| Handle | same | SemiBold | 26 px | @videogamers |

- Text color **#0F0F0F** on white. No outlines, shadows or gradients on text (except the classic Impact meme format, used rarely).
- **Spelling must be perfect.** If the generator garbles text, generate the image without text and add the text after.

## 7. Art style

**Wojak-style editorial cartoon.**
- Clean, confident **bold black outlines** (uniform weight, ~3–4px at 1080).
- **Flat colors**, minimal soft shading, no gradients on characters, no texture, no painterly effects.
- **Pure white background.** No rooms or walls unless the joke needs them (a doorway for mom, a desk for work).
- Faces: classic wojak. Simple, pale, slightly crude, expressive eyes. **Never** realistic or anime.
- Props: drawn cleanly and recognizably, like a product illustration. Logos and labels readable.
- Lighting: none, flat and even. The 2026 panel can add a TV glow or dark-mode blue tint if the joke is "late night".

## 8. Character bible

### The Guy (2026, age 25), the main character
- Short brown hair, **short beard**, **dark circles**, slightly red tired eyes, faint "it is what it is" half-smile.
- **Work fit:** navy fleece vest over a light-blue checked button-up, khakis, loafers.
- **Night fit:** gray hoodie, sweatpants, slides, headset around his neck.
- Sits in a gaming chair (lumbar pillow), on a couch, or at a desk with a laptop.
- Expressions: tired satisfaction, dead-eyed focus, quiet defeat, "gg".

### The Kid (him, ages 12–16, 2009–2014)
- **Flat-brim snapback** (backwards or straight), **bright tee** (lime green, red, or a Nike swoosh), basketball shorts, Nike Elite socks.
- Sits **cross-legged on the carpet** or on a **bean bag**, too close to the TV.
- Expressions: big happy wojak grin, begging hands (praying), screaming at the TV.

### Supporting cast (use sparingly)
| Character | Look | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Mom** (2010) | in the doorway, arms crossed, holding a laundry basket or cordless phone | "DINNER", "get off the game", unplugging the Xbox |
| **Girlfriend** (2026) | on the couch under a blanket, phone in hand, Netflix/reality TV glow | "come to bed", "what's so special about this one" |
| **The boys** | shown as party chat bubbles / friends list entries, not drawn | 2010: 12 online. 2026: "last online 47 days ago" |
| **The 12-year-old** | smug, braces, hoodie, headset too big for him | the killcam that ends him |
| **The boss** | only ever a Teams/Outlook notification, never a face | the 9am |

**Never** draw real people (streamers, celebrities, Rockstar staff).

## 9. Prop library (use era-accurate items only)

### Childhood panel (2009–2014)
**Consoles & gear:** Xbox 360 (white original or black slim), PS3 slim, Wii, Nintendo DS, iPod Touch, iPad (for Clash), Turtle Beach X12 (cord plugged into the controller), Xbox 360 controller with AA batteries, Play & Charge kit, 32" TV on a dresser
**Games (cases):** MW2, Black Ops, MW3, Black Ops II, Halo Reach, GTA V (2013), Skyrim, Assassin's Creed II / Brotherhood, Minecraft (Xbox 360 edition), NBA 2K13
**Cards & money:** Xbox Live Gold 12-month card, 1600 Microsoft Points card, a GameStop receipt, a $20 birthday card from grandma
**Food & drink:** Mtn Dew (Game Fuel), Doritos Nacho Cheese, Hot Pockets, Pizza Rolls, Capri Sun, Gatorade, large pepperoni pizza
**Other:** Disney XD / Nickelodeon on the TV, a Nerf gun, a school backpack on the floor, a homework sheet (untouched), a flip phone or iPhone 4, a clock reading 11:47pm, a "to do list" sticky note

### Adult panel (2026)
**Consoles & gear:** PS5 / PS5 Pro, DualSense, Xbox Series X, wireless headset, OLED TV, Steam Deck, gaming chair with lumbar pillow
**Games:** GTA VI case, Black Ops 7, NBA 2K26, a Steam library screen ("23 games · 0 hours")
**Work:** laptop with Teams/Outlook open, Excel, "9:00 AM standup" notification, mouse jiggler, a lanyard/badge, PTO calendar with **Nov 19** circled
**Body:** ibuprofen, Epsom salt, a heating pad, blue-light glasses, Apple Watch "time to wind down", sleep score 54
**Food & drink:** White Monster (Ultra), Celsius, Michelob Ultra, DoorDash bag, the same Doritos (the constant)
**Other:** phone on Do Not Disturb, a bills folder, a car key, a clock reading 11:52pm (he's asleep in the lobby)

**Pairing trick:** keep 1–2 props *identical* across both panels (the Doritos, the pizza, the 11:47 clock). It makes the comparison obvious.

```
Wojak-style cartoon illustration, clean bold black outlines, flat colors, pure white background, no text.
[CHARACTER: kid version | adult version, from the character bible], [POSE/ACTION], [EXPRESSION].
Surrounding props arranged in a clean flat-lay, clearly separated and readable: [PROP 1], [PROP 2], ... [PROP 8].

```

## 11. Captions

**Long-form, 100–200 words. A second joke, not a summary.** Stonks uses finance-bro jargon; **we use gamer / patch-notes language about adult life.**

**Structure:**
1. **Opener in patch-notes voice:** "In 2013, the main quest was getting GTA V without a parent reading the back of the case."
2. **Specific numbers:** 11:52pm, 47.3 GB, $11.40, 18 days, "three games". Odd numbers are funnier.
3. **An escalating run of adult details:** Teams status, Epsom salt, falling asleep in the lobby.
4. **One sincere turn:** "He didn't outgrow the perfect night. The perfect night just got a recovery protocol."
5. **Parody disclaimer:** "Rated M for Mature. Online interactions not rated by the ESRB." / "Progress will not be saved." / "Do not turn off your console while the 25-year-old is saving."
6. **CTA:** "Follow @videogamers for more ___" (rotate it: "…content you'll see at 1am instead of sleeping", "We're also not making it to the 30th.")
7. **Hashtags (4–5):** #videogamers #gamingmemes #nostalgia + 1–2 specific (#gta6 #blackops2 #fortnite #clashofclans)

**Vocabulary bank:** main quest, side quest, nerfed, buffed, stamina bar, respawn timer, patch notes, AFK, ping, lag, DLC, season pass, server maintenance, save file, hard difficulty, eliminated, wasted, gg, lobby, party chat, killcam, XP, level up, prestige.

## 13. Quality gates (every post)
- [ ] He'd send it to the group chat with no caption
- [ ] The viewer was the kid **and** is the adult
- [ ] One constant, one variable
- [ ] 6+ specific, era-accurate props per panel
- [ ] Gaming is the subject
- [ ] Not a premise @stonks already did (perfect night 2012, GTA V→VI wallet, $60 vs 45 minutes, american dream, book fair)
- [ ] Text spelled correctly, readable at phone size
- [ ] The caption is its own joke, ending with a disclaimer + CTA
