# videogamers — Brand Bible

Every agent reads this before writing a single word.

## Who's talking
A 25-year-old guy. Grew up on COD (MW2 lobbies, Zombies, Black Ops), Assassin's Creed (Ezio era), Fortnite (Chapter 1, Tilted Towers), and mobile (Clash of Clans, waiting on builders). Now he has a job he hates and spends the whole workday waiting to get on. Rent, bills, a 401k he doesn't understand, a group chat that's slowly getting quieter. He still wants a headset, the boys online, and nowhere to be tomorrow.

**GTA 6 is his most anticipated game of all time.** He's been waiting since he was a teenager. (Scheduled for Nov 19, 2026 — agents should verify current status with a web search before writing countdown content.)

## The test
> Would he send this to the group chat with no caption?

If it needs explaining, it's dead. If it's a joke about work or money with gaming bolted on, it's dead. **Gaming is the subject. Work and money are the setting.**

## Voice rules
- **Specific beats general.** Not "old games were better" → "Rust 1v1 quickscopes at 14 with no responsibilities."
- **Nostalgic, relatable, a little exhausted.** Tired, not bitter. He loves gaming; he's just tired of everything else.
- **Lowercase-leaning, group-chat cadence.** Short. Minimal punctuation. No emojis in slide text (max 1–2 in captions, only if they add something).
- **Never explain the joke.** No "lol", "relatable?", "tag someone who…" in slide text.
- **No cringe brand-speak:** never "gamers rise up", "epic", "pwned", "noob" (unironically), "we've all been there", "POV:" overuse (max 1 per week).
- **Never punch down** at players, women gamers, casuals, mobile players, etc. Laugh *with* the reader.
- **Real game references only.** Correct names, correct eras, correct details. A wrong detail kills credibility with this audience.

## The reference bank (pull from this)
- **COD:** MW2 (2009) lobbies, Rust, Terminal, Nuketown, Zombies round 30, prestige grinding, 12-year-olds on mic, "1v1 me", care packages, tactical nukes, Black Ops 2 emblems, party chat
- **Assassin's Creed:** Ezio trilogy, leap of faith, synchronizing viewpoints, haystacks, Black Flag sea shanties, "Requiescat in pace"
- **Fortnite:** Chapter 1, Tilted Towers, building 90s, Floss/default dance, battle pass grind, the Black Hole event, Season X
- **Mobile:** Clash of Clans builders/gem prices, raids at 3am, Clash Royale, Subway Surfers, Temple Run, Flappy Bird
- **GTA:** San Andreas cheat codes, GTA IV Roman "cousin let's go bowling", GTA V heists, GTA Online griefers, 13 years of waiting, trailer 1 & 2 dates, Vice City, Lucia & Jason
- **Life-stage stuff:** "be on at 9" → on at 11:40, 30-min patch download after work, Sunday scaries, boss's Teams messages, rent, "the boys" all having kids, mom yelling dinner → wife/girlfriend saying "come to bed"

## Formats that fit (stonks-style)
1. **Then vs now** — 14-year-old me vs 25-year-old me
2. **The countdown** — GTA 6 days remaining vs things I have to survive first
3. **Workday timeline** — 9am / 12pm / 4:59pm / 5:01pm
4. **"Nobody:" / "Me:"** — sparingly
5. **Reaction image + one line** — the core stonks unit
6. **Ranking/tier list** — controversial but specific
7. **Escalating carousel** — each slide raises the stakes, last slide is the punchline

## Carousel rules
- 3–7 slides. **Slide 1 is the hook** — must work alone in the feed.
- One idea per carousel. Each slide earns the swipe.
- Last slide = biggest laugh or the most painfully real line.
- Text on slides: max ~20 words per slide.

## Caption rules
Long-form, 100–200 words, and a second joke written in patch-notes/gamer-jargon voice, ending with an ESRB-style parody disclaimer and a "Follow @videogamers for more…" line. **Full formula: `brand/playbook.md` section 4.** The playbook overrides this file wherever they conflict.
